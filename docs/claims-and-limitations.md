# Claims and Limitations

## Current claim

Blackwall Core applies default-deny policy decisions to requests submitted through its experimental protocol.

## Product direction

Blackwall is designed to stop unauthorized AI actions at the boundaries it enforces.

## Language reserved for later evidence

“Sandbox,” “contains an agent,” “blocks file access,” “blocks network access,” “real-time protection,” and “tamper protection” require an OS-enforced implementation and adversarial validation. “Stops every AI,” “unbreakable,” and “prevents all AI attacks” are not acceptable claims.

An allow decision means that an exact policy grant matched. It is not a safety verdict. A deny decision has no effect on software that can bypass the policy endpoint.
